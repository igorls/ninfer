HOW TO PLAY

Click a piece, then a highlighted square. Choose a piece explicitly when promoting. Castling, en passant and draw detection use chess.js.

Choose Jev vs Qwen for a model matchup. Each side has its own connection, warm-up and measurements. Swap sides resets the starting position and preserves the connections. Choose the local reference to try the board without a server.

System One sees the position and every legal move, with captures, checkmate and attacked-piece facts. The browser never replaces its choice with the reference's move.

Up to 62 moves use one request. Larger move sets use piece selection followed by move selection; both requests and conditional probabilities appear in the inspector.

Arrow keys navigate the board; Enter selects. P pauses. Move history supports replay. The model's probabilities are preferences, not win probabilities.
