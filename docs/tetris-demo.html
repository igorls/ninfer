HOW IT WORKS

The browser enumerates straight-drop landings and measures each resulting board. System One chooses a letter; its response supplies the full probability distribution.

Policy: most lines, then fewest holes, lowest stack, least roughness. Agreement compares that policy exactly, including ties. It does not measure optimal Tetris play.

This is a placement challenge: no wall kicks, tucks, hold, or movement-path search. The falling piece shows the time budget. A valid answer selects a top-drop destination.

Manual: ← → choose column, ↑ rotate, Space place. Buttons also work on touchscreens. P pauses; R resets.
