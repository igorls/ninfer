HOW TO PLAY

Collect → chop → cook → plate → serve. Salad skips cooking. Each chef handles one job at a time; a reserved order or station cannot be assigned twice.

Cooking continues without a chef. Plate cooked food within five seconds or it burns. Clear a burned pan to reopen the stove. Each ticket has its own service deadline.

Cooperate gives each player one chef in a shared kitchen. Both see their partner's work and can continue the same dish. Simultaneous claims may conflict: real time accepts the first valid answer, while paused rounds rotate chef priority. Rejected jobs are shown; the chef chooses again.

Select You to choose jobs below your player's readout. Walking and task execution are automatic. P pauses. Paused decision mode and Step make manual play easier.

Compare gives each player a separate kitchen with identical seeded arrivals, recipes and deadlines. The local reference is a greedy scheduling baseline, not an optimal strategy. Probabilities express action preferences.
